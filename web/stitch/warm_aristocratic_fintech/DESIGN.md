## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an aristocratic, tranquil, and hyper-reliable aesthetic tailored for private wealth management and AI-guided personal finance. Grounded in the motto *"Count. Plan. Protect."*, the visual architecture rejects aggressive synthetic neon gradients and clinical neo-brutalist austerity in favor of an editorial, warm-heritage finish described as **"Soft. Rich. Timeless."**

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** High-net-worth individuals, discerning professionals, and meticulous planners seeking high-conviction AI financial foresight without cognitive fatigue.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Quiet confidence, private banking exclusivity, sovereign control, and enduring structural calm. Every element communicates safety, deliberate calculation, and bespoke care.

### Design Movement & Language
The design system fuses **High-End Editorial Typography** with **Tactile Warm Modernism**:
- Deep, foundational dark navy structural planes anchor dynamic canvases draped in creamy, tactile almond and warm ivory.
- Micro-accents in soft muted gold impart intrinsic value without garish friction.
- Geometry balances crisp structural integrity with gently eased corners, subtle border separations, and luminous, ambient paper-like depth.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative structure with tactile warmth, intentionally rejecting pure digital whites (`#FFFFFF`) and harsh pure blacks (`#000000`).

### Core Hierarchy
- **Primary — Night Sky Navy (`#0B1F44`):** The definitive anchor. Applied to the persistent command sidebar, authoritative editorial display titles, primary interactive states, and dense structural elements.
- **Secondary — Soft Gold (`#C9A227`):** The signature wealth accent. Reserved for high-value AI insights, active filter pills, balance focal points, key action triggers, and primary progress indicators.
- **Surface Canvas — Almond Beige (`#E8DCC6`):** The global viewport background, offering a serene, linen-like visual resting space that reduces screen glare.
- **Surface Container — Light Beige (`#F5EFE4`):** The primary card and widget canvas, elevating actionable information above the Almond ground.
- **Structural Stroke — Muted Linen (`#DDD0B8`):** Ultra-refined hairline borders framing cards, divider rules, and input perimeters.

### Semantic & Status System
- **Affirmative / Safe (`#2A9D8F`):** Deep sea pine green. Indicates surplus capital, authenticated accounts, and safe threshold statuses.
- **Caution / Warning (`#D9922B`):** Warm honey amber. Flags upcoming amortizations, volatile liquidity fluctuations, and approaching category caps.
- **Critical / Danger (`#8B1E2D`):** Rich vintage burgundy. Emphasizes overdrawn balances, security threats, and emergency liquidity events.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances aristocratic heritage with high-precision analytical readability.

### Type Pairings
- **Display & Headings:** `Playfair Display`. Evokes vintage financial gazettes, classic institutions, and sovereign prestige. Used exclusively for section titles, portfolio summaries, and conversational AI insights.
- **Interface & Data Numerics:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Imparts modern legibility, balanced proportions, and clean tabular numerals.

### Numerical Treatment
All quantitative monetary displays, stock tickers, and ledger balances must be rendered with OpenType tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). This guarantees columnar alignment across complex financial data grids and AI projection matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **Structured Fluid Hybrid Model**, balancing an unyielding command sidebar with a flexible, modular viewport.

### Shell Architecture
- **Desktop Sidebar:** Fixed width of `280px` anchored flush to the left, rendered in deep Night Sky navy (`#0B1F44`).
- **Main Viewport Canvas:** Fluid remaining width on Almond beige (`#E8DCC6`), scaling with a constrained maximum content width of `1600px` to maintain optimal data scanning density.
- **Card Grid:** Fluid 12-column layout across desktop contexts with standard `1.5rem` gutters. Elements reflow into 6 columns for tablet viewports and a unified single-column stream under `768px`.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** The dark sidebar collapses into an ambient bottom utility bar or off-canvas drawer. Viewport margins tighten to `margin-mobile` (`1rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** The sidebar transforms into an 80px micro-icon rail. Viewport margins scale to `margin` (`2rem`).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Full persistent 280px sidebar. Viewport margins widen to `margin-desktop` (`2.5rem`), allowing cards to breathe with generous internal gutters.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids harsh synthetic drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering combined with Ambient Warm Diffusion**. Surfaces sit upon one another like heavy, premium cotton rag paper.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Base Layer (Level 0):** Canvas backdrop (`#E8DCC6`). Raw, non-elevated foundation.
2. **Surface Layer (Level 1):** Primary cards, analytical widgets, and ledger modules (`#F5EFE4`). Outlined by a crisp `1px` border in `#DDD0B8` and accompanied by a soft, diffused shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 31, 68, 0.04)`.
3. **Floating Tier (Level 2):** Dropdowns, popovers, and interactive flyouts (`#F5EFE4`). Outlined by `1px solid #DDD0B8`, elevated by an ambient spread: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 31, 68, 0.08)`.
4. **Modal Overlays (Level 3):** Deep dialogue boxes with a backdrop scrim set to `#0B1F44` at `40%` opacity with a subtle `4px` blur. Modals project an authoritative shadow: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(11, 31, 68, 0.16)`.
5. **Persistent Command Sidebar:** Flat, deep Night Sky navy (`#0B1F44`) with no external shadow, separating itself purely through crisp visual tonality and a hairline right border in `rgba(232, 220, 198, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape vocabulary, pairing structural discipline with organic tactility.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Micro Elements (Tags, Indicators, Badges):** `rounded` (`0.5rem`). Keeps small elements structured without appearing clinical.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Standard Buttons):** `rounded` (`0.5rem`). Conveys tactile precision under tap or click.
- **Containers & Surface Modules (Cards, Financial Panels):** `rounded-lg` (`1rem`). Softens high-density data tables and balances analytical charts.
- **Overlays, Large Sheets, and Dialog Modals:** `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`). Creates distinct separation from the rigid screen frame.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Metric status tags and active filter chips utilize full pill radii (`9999px`) to create organic micro-accents within dense grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Protect" Action):** Background `#0B1F44`, text `#F5EFE4`, border `1px solid #0B1F44`. On hover, subtly lightens to `#132B5B` with an understated warm gold focus ring (`2px solid #C9A227`).
- **Accent ("Plan" Action):** Background `#C9A227`, text `#0B1F44` (bold), border none. Used sparingly for key AI conversion flows and primary balance reallocation triggers.
- **Secondary (Outline):** Background transparent, text `#0B1F44`, border `1px solid #DDD0B8`. On hover, surface fills to `#E8DCC6`.

### Cards & Financial Widgets
- **Standard Card:** Background `#F5EFE4`, border `1px solid #DDD0B8`, border radius `1rem`, inner padding `1.5rem`.
- **AI Insight Card:** Background `#F5EFE4`, featuring an inset left accent bar in Soft Gold (`3px solid #C9A227`). Typography within mixes `Playfair Display` italic phrases with tabular numeral highlights.

### Chips & Micro-Badges
- **Status Badges:** Compact footprint with `0.25rem 0.5rem` padding, font size `0.75rem`, weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`.
  - *Affirmative:* Background `rgba(42, 157, 143, 0.12)`, text `#2A9D8F`.
  - *Warning:* Background `rgba(217, 146, 43, 0.14)`, text `#D9922B`.
  - *Danger:* Background `rgba(139, 30, 45, 0.12)`, text `#8B1E2D`.
- **Gold Micro-Badge:** Background `rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.15)`, text `#8C6E15`, border `1px solid rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.3)`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height `2.75rem`, background `#F5EFE4`, border `1px solid #DDD0B8`, radius `0.5rem`, text `#0B1F44`. Active focus creates a smooth transition to border `#0B1F44` with a subtle gold glow ring (`2px rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.25)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Border `1.5px solid #DDD0B8`, background `#F5EFE4`. Checked state fills with `#0B1F44` presenting a crisp `#F5EFE4` checkmark.

### Lists & Ledger Rows
- Interactive ledger rows utilize `1px solid #DDD0B8` separators with zero horizontal padding margins. Hover transitions reveal an ambient wash of `#E8DCC6` at `50%` opacity. Transaction titles are styled in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold, while values are aligned with tabular numerals.
